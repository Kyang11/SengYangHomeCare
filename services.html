Home Care Services

Personal Care Assistance

Having someone reliable, respectful, and competent at home is invaluable. Our PCAs help with the daily essentials so clients can live with dignity and ease.

  • Bathing & toileting
  • Dressing & clothes selection
  • Grooming & oral hygiene
  • Transferring & mobility
  • Ambulation & fall prevention
  • Wheelchair assistance
🍲

  • Laundering
  • Grocery shopping & meal preparation
  • Cooking & feeding

Individualized Home Supports (IHS)

Minnesota waiver service for people with disabilities providing support and training in community participation, health & wellness, household management, and adaptive skills. Available on BI, CAC, CADI, and DD waivers.

  • Support & training in 4 community living categories
  • Delivered in person's own home, family home, or community
  • Three tiers: without training, with training, with family training
  • In-person or remote delivery
👵

Individual Community Living Supports (ICLS)

Minnesota waiver service for older adults (65+) providing support with cognitive support, ADLs, household management, health & wellness, and community engagement. Available on Elderly Waiver (EW) and Alternative Care (AC).

  • Support in 6 areas including cognitive & adaptive support
  • Delivered in person's own home, family home, or community
  • In-person or remote delivery
  • Provider cannot be landlord; participant owns/rents home
