Our Mission

Helping loved ones stay home, where they belong.

Individuals with disabilities and older adults can face challenges in their daily lives. At Seng Yang Home Care, we bridge that gap with trained Personal Care Assistants who help clients participate in everyday activities just like everyone else.

We strive to keep clients in their own homes for as long as possible, avoiding the need for nursing homes or assisted living. We prioritize the prevention of illness and the promotion of health, supporting each client's potential for optimal well-being.

What We Do

Care that fits each need

Every client is unique. Our PCAs deliver tailored support, from daily living assistance to wellness reminders and overnight peace of mind.
