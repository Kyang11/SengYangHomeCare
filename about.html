About Seng Yang Home Care

Family-rooted, community-driven, and dedicated to helping loved ones stay at home, where memories live.

Seng Yang Home Care has proudly served as a community services agency since 20XX (example). As a non-medical home health agency, our mission is to provide Personal Care Assistants (PCAs) who support individuals with physical or cognitive disabilities, as well as older adults, in their daily activities.

We know that selecting the right home health agency for your loved one can feel overwhelming. That's why we ensure our Personal Care Workers receive thorough training and orientation. They are equipped to deliver compassionate, high-quality care tailored to the unique needs of each client.

Our Mission

To deliver the highest quality of care to our clients — ensuring they receive compassionate and comprehensive home care services that keep them in their own homes for as long as possible, avoiding the need for nursing homes or assisted living facilities.

Our Values

Compassion

We treat every client like family. Dignity, respect, and warmth guide every visit.
